Questions

Can I use Vikri without internet?

Yes. Daily billing works on your device. Cloud backup and sync need internet and a paid plan. Account and device checks may also need internet.

What happens when a paid plan ends?

Your data stays on your device. Your first phone goes back to billing with ads. Other paid devices can only view records. Cloud backup and sync stop until you renew.
