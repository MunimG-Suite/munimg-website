Can I use Vikri without internet?

Yes. Daily billing works on your device. Cloud backup and sync need internet and active paid access. Account and device checks may also need internet.

What happens when a paid plan ends?

Your local data stays on your device. The designated first phone returns to billing with ads. Other paid devices become read-only. Cloud backup and sync pause until paid access resumes.
