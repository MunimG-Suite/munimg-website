Request account deletion.

Contact MunimG Solutions to request removal of your Vikri account and associated data. You can make a request without signing in to Vikri or installing it again.

What should I tell support?

Say that you want to delete your Vikri account. Give the email address you use for Vikri. Do not send your PIN, password, OTP or backup recovery key.

What happens next?

Support will check that the account belongs to you and review the data connected to it. Contacting support does not immediately delete your data. The details must be confirmed before removal.

What about my business records?

Tell support which business records are yours and whether other people share them. Shared business records need a separate review. Save a copy of records you need before asking for deletion.

What can remain after deletion?

Files on your phone, computer or in exports are not removed by a server request. If information must be kept by law or because it belongs to a shared business, support must explain what is kept and why.
