3. Local operation and cloud synchronization

A free-only company's business books remain in its local workspace unless you choose an export, sharing or another disclosed transfer. Local operation can still use the internet for sign-in, licence checks, referral checks, updates, support and connected features.

When a company's cloud mode is enabled, eligible business records and changes are sent to our Supabase-backed service and made available to that company's authorised users and devices. These can include customer details and financial records. The server processes these structured records. This is different from storing an encrypted backup file. Cloud synchronization is not described as end-to-end encryption, where only the users can read the contents.

Company permissions control user access. The business owner must grant access with care. Removing access cannot recall every copy that a former user already exported or stored elsewhere. Our staff access is limited as described in section 7.

4. Backups, files and exports

Local/PC backups and Vikri Cloud backups are separate storage destinations.

  • Free local backups: MunimG Solutions does not encrypt this file format. A person who obtains the file can read its business contents. Protect the file and device.
  • Trial/paid backups: the protected file format uses encryption. You still need the file and the required access or recovery material. Encryption cannot recover a lost file or missing recovery material.
  • Vikri Cloud backups: the service accepts the encrypted backup format and processes details needed to check, control and restore it. This does not make the separate synchronization records unreadable to the server.
  • Exports and sharing: PDFs, spreadsheets, images, prints and messages can reveal personal and financial information. Check contents and recipients before sharing. Recipients can keep copies outside our control.

Managed local backups, made manually or automatically, keep one checked copy for each of the latest three India calendar days with successful backups. A later successful copy replaces the earlier copy for the same day. The latest two Safety Backups, made before a restore, are kept separately. An India calendar day uses India Standard Time.

The Vikri Cloud backup policy keeps the latest completed copy from each of the latest two India calendar days with a successful backup, plus one rolling monthly copy. The monthly copy is the last successful copy from the most recent completed India calendar month that had a backup. These are company copies shared by its authorised devices. A file used for both daily and monthly purposes is stored once. A replacement must be checked and available before the older copy is removed. A failed upload keeps the previous successful copy.

The App PIN is optional. A copied encrypted file needs its original device key or a PIN saved into that file. An authorised replacement device can instead recover current cloud company records through synchronization, where that access is available. This may give current synchronized records rather than the historical contents of a particular backup file.

Files copied outside managed storage follow that storage owner's controls. A failed backup or offline device affects which copies exist. Keep and test the independent copies needed for your business and legal records. Section 8 covers deletion and retention after connected access ends.

5. Optional permissions, voice input and device checks

The app requests permissions for selected features, subject to the operating system's controls. These can include contacts, camera, microphone, notifications, Bluetooth/nearby devices and files. You can deny or revoke optional permissions in the device or provider settings. The related feature may then be unavailable.

Voice input starts when you choose it and uses the device's speech-recognition service. That service may send audio to its provider and may need the internet. MunimG Solutions does not operate that speech service or send raw dictation audio to its own server through this feature. Review the recognised text before applying it. If you save it as a party or item name, it becomes normal business data. You can type instead.

Fingerprint or face checks use the operating system. MunimG Solutions receives the check result, rather than your fingerprint or face template. Protect your device and App PIN. Giving another person device access can reduce the protection of local information.

How to request deletion

  1. WhatsApp or call +91 92 25 25 25 66 using the contacts in section 1. Say: “I want to delete my Vikri account.” You can also write to the postal address. You do not need to keep or reinstall the app, remain signed in, or buy or renew a plan merely to make a request.
  2. Give the Google sign-in email used for Vikri and a safe way to reply. If you want a company deleted too, give its name and explain your role. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, PINs, Aadhaar documents, card details or entire customer lists through ordinary support.
  3. We check that the account belongs to you and that you have authority for the requested company action. A phone call or WhatsApp number alone is not proof of ownership. We explain any further check and use only proportionate information needed to prevent an unauthorised deletion.
  4. We explain the effect on sign-in, paid access, company users, hosted records and backups. Where a shared company is affected, we check other users' rights and any necessary transfer or removal of access. We do not refuse all deletion merely because some records are shared.
  5. We delete the account and associated personal data within our control, and instruct service providers where required. Any specific information kept for a lawful reason is limited to that reason. We tell you what was removed, what must remain, why it remains and the applicable period or end condition. We also explain relevant local copies and any pending provider removal.

This is a support-handled request. Sending a message or opening a page does not automatically erase data. We handle the request without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines, tell you the expected steps and timing, and explain a delay or a refusal. Temporary suspension or freezing is not a substitute for deletion.

Uninstalling, ending a plan, revoking Google access, deleting a Google account, removing a local file and asking MunimG Solutions to delete an account are different actions. None automatically performs all the others. We do not remotely erase a local business database merely because a plan or account ends. We assist with removal within our control, while explaining copies or lawful duties outside it.

10. Security and incidents

We must use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and applicable law. These include company access controls, protection of account sessions, and the backup protections described above. Security also depends on the device, network, provider configuration and staff controls. No system offers perfect security, and not all data is end-to-end encrypted.

Report suspected unauthorised access using section 1. Do not send secret access or recovery material. We investigate incidents and provide the notices and cooperation required by applicable law. The Terms do not remove mandatory security or notification duties.

11. Children and information entered by businesses

Vikri accounts are intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not invite children to create accounts. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied account information so that we can assess and act on it.

A business must collect and use other people's information lawfully, give required notices and restrict access. Do not enter unnecessary information about children or other people. These business duties do not remove MunimG Solutions' own duties. A child's information remains protected when an adult enters it.
